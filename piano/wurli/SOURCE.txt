The piano's "Wurlitzer" sound (2 Oct 2026, patches/piano-sounds.py): Wurlitzer EP200 Electric Piano v1.1 by Greg Sullivan,
from "Greg Sullivan's E-Pianos" mapped by kinwie. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos

LICENCE: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0) - credit required: "Wurlitzer EP200 by Greg Sullivan, CC BY 3.0"
(shown in the piano's menu while this sound is chosen).

What is here: every region of the library's map - 25 zones, four loudness layers (1-37, 38-65, 66-89, 90-127) - its tune
baked in; a layer recorded on another note than its zone's centre retuned to it; mono MP3 112 kbps, 7 s at most with a
0.8 s fade (_lab/epiano-listen/fetch_epianos.py). map.json: the guitar's format, read by gtrTake.
Chosen by David on the electric piano listening page (_lab/epiano-listen/).
