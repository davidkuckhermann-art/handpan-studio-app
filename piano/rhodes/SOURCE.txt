The piano's "Rhodes" sound (2 Oct 2026, patches/piano-sounds.py): jRhodes GM by Jeff Learman - a 1977 Rhodes Mark I Stage 73,
the Discord SFZ General MIDI Bank's "005-Electric Piano 1" (mapped and relooped for GM by jlearman).
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/Discord-SFZ-GM-Bank - source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3c

LICENCE: CC0 (stated in the SFZ: "License: Creative Commons CC0, Jeff Learman"). No credit needed, no limits on use inside the
app. (The full jRhodes3c/3d libraries are CC BY-NC - not these files.)

What is here: the GM map's 15 zones, one layer each; each looped sample played out as a sampler plays it (the attack, then its
loop, the region's hold and decay), mono MP3 112 kbps, 7 s with a 0.8 s fade (_lab/epiano-listen/fetch_epianos.py).
map.json: [{lo, hi, c, layers: [[lovel, hivel, [files]]]}] - the guitar's format, read by gtrTake.
Chosen by David on the electric piano listening page (_lab/epiano-listen/).
