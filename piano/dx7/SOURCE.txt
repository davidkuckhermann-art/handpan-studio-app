The piano's "DX7" sound (2 Oct 2026, patches/piano-sounds.py): FreePats FM Piano 1 - an imitation of the Yamaha DX7 electric
piano, recorded from the Hexter software synthesizer. https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ElectricPiano/synthesized-piano.html
(https://github.com/freepats/fm-piano1, release 2019-09-16, FM-Piano1-SF2-20190916.7z, preset 000-000 "FM Piano 1").

LICENCE: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). No credit needed, no limits on use inside the app.

What is here: the preset's 12 zones, three loudness layers, pulled with sf2_extract.py (its loop and volume envelope), mono
MP3 112 kbps, 7 s with a 0.8 s fade. map.json: the guitar's format, read by gtrTake.
Chosen by David on the electric piano listening page (_lab/epiano-listen/).
