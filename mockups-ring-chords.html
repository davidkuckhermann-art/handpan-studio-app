Ring, chord view: the chords beside the pan

The landscape pan sheet in chord mode, five ways, each drawn at the phone's own sideways size (812 × 375). The pan is the app's own drawing with the chord's fields lit; the boxes are the chord tab's boxes. × top left, ⚙ top right in every one. Opened on the phone this page shows each screen at half size — turn the phone sideways for the truest look.
