Bottom-note badge in the notation

Today the badge is a fill and nothing else. The marker lays a warm wash over the badge and its slot equally, so it squeezes whatever difference the fill had. On a banded beat the badge measures 1.05 against its slot, where 1.00 means identical.

An edge does not wash out. That is the difference between the options below, more than the hue is.

Every colour is the app's own token. Every number is computed from the same values that paint the swatches. Scroll: each option is shown on all three score sheets, with a plain slot, a normal note, a slot under the marker, and a banded beat.
