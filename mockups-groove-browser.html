Groove browser — categories and favourites

A working mockup: the tabs filter, the hearts stick, the family headings fold. ♥ sits first — the same heart the pan picker taught — and a groove may appear under more than one tab, because "Backbeat arpeggio" is both things and should be findable as either. Collections keep doing the grouping inside whichever tab is open. Category assignments are a draft; corrections at the foot of the page.

What building it involves

Small: a cats:[…] field beside the coll the grooves already carry, favourites in hps.grooveFavs exactly as pan favourites are stored, and the tab row in the one renderer both groove dialogs share. User-written grooves can be hearted too and appear under ♥ and All (a category picker in the New-groove dialog could come later, or never). The selected tab is remembered, so the browser reopens where you left it.
