Groove browser

Forty-three grooves, browsable. This is the scale selector's design with the content swapped — same frame, same filter row, same rows and hairlines, same clay heart — so nothing new has to be learned. The one new idea is that a family is one row with variation chips, exactly as one scale is one row with build chips: the list is twenty-nine rows instead of forty-three, and “Relaxed backbeat 1–5” reads as one groove with variations.

Everything below is live. Change the filter, tap a chip, heart a family — the states are real, not drawn.

What this proposes, and what to overturn

A family is one row

The pan picker had already solved this problem: one scale is one row, and its builds are chips inside it — 9 · 10 · 17. A groove collection is the same shape, so “Relaxed backbeat” is one row with chips 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5. Forty-three grooves become twenty-nine rows — eight families and twenty-one singles. The subtitle carries the meter and the count: 4/4 · 5 variations. Tapping a chip both chooses the variation and selects the family, so one tap is still one groove.

The heart marks the family

As the picker's heart marks the scale and not the build. Hearting “Relaxed backbeat” keeps the whole family in ♥ with its chips intact — you favourite the groove you like, not the fifth variation of it.

♥ sits last in the filter row

You asked for “a favourites tab at the top”. The pan picker puts its heart last, after Common · Rare · All. I followed the picker for consistency, since that is what this pass is for — but this is the one place the two readings disagree, and moving it to the front is a one-line change. Say which.

A groove may carry several categories and appears under each — “Backbeat arpeggio” is both, and filing it once would hide it from whoever looks under the other name.

Building it

Small, because almost nothing is new: the picker's CSS is reused, a cats:[…] field joins the coll the grooves already carry, favourites store as hps.grooveFavs beside the pan favourites, and the family rows come from the patGroups() the browser already computes. The chosen filter is remembered so the browser reopens where it was left. ▸ previews the groove on the current pan — the picker has it, this never did.
