The three recorded basses are free to use in the app (CC0 - no licence to settle): the VSCO 2 Community Edition contrabass, and Karoryfer's Meatbass and Sneakybass - one 1958 double bass, played with a full pluck and played very quietly. One note about every minor third was taken from each (about 350 KB a bass); the notes between are the nearest one at another speed. Each is levelled to the same loudness, so switching compares the sound, not the volume. Ring: a note rings until the next one stops it, as in the app. Short: every note is damped at half its length.
