Handpan Studio · phone

One drawer, four rooms

The same drawer in every room, in the same order: the transport line on top, then the one thing this room is about, then your pan, then feel. Drawn at true size — 375 points wide, the app's real button sizes — so what you see here is what fits on the phone.

The two open questions — flip them and look

The shared shape

Line 1 — the transport, identical in every room. Tempo, count-in, half speed, metronome, marker on the left; play on the right. No heading: you know what these are.
Line 2 — what this room is. The arrangement navigator when you are reading or writing a piece; the chord voicings when you are jamming; the groove's own tools on the bench.
Line 3 — my pan. Instrument, and whether it sounds as written or transposed.
Line 4 — feel. Swing.
They are the same buttons, moved. When the drawer opens, play, the tempo and the arrangement chips leave the resting bar and come up into it; on close they drop back. That is what the editor already does with its writing deck, so all four rooms now behave the same way.
