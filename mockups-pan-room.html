Where the air under the pan came from — before

In Tab, at this geometry, the pan card stood 6px from the dots. Everything else you saw as "gap" was reserved space:

  • Fitter reserve, first (space = … − 10)10 px
  • Fitter reserve, second (leftover = … − 10)10 px
  • Dots strip — 7px of dot inside 9px above + 3px below19 px
  • Pan card's own bottom padding8 px
  • The drawing's margin below the rim (room for underside notes)≈ 30 px
  • Reads as one gap of about≈ 77 px

And the Tab card was 42px taller than the Table's — four rows of cells with 8px above and 6px below their glyphs. That 42px is what the pan lost: 281 → 253px.

What the change does — after

  • Tab cell air on the portrait phone, 8/6 → 5/4 — four rows−20 px card
  • Dots strip, padding 9/3 → 2/1 (David: "bring the dot strip down to 10 px")19 → 10 px
  • Fitter reserves, 10 → 8, both20 → 16 px
  • Pan card padding, drawing marginunchanged
  • Room handed back to the pan in Tab+33 px

Tab pan 253 → 280px against the Table's width cap of 281 — to a pixel, the Table's pan. I tried 4: the pan card sat 4px over the dots, because the 14px margin between the cards and the score's border aren't in the fitter's measure. So 8 is the floor that was measured, not guessed.

Desktop, landscape and Flow read none of this: the cell padding is scoped to the portrait phone, and the reserves only matter where the pan is height-bound.
