The decision this page settled (David, 27 Aug): A wins. In A the top voice walks F4 → E4 → F4 — a step down and back. In B it jumps F4 → C5 → F4, so A minor lifts out of the phrase and lands back. Wide still sounds beautiful alone, so the app now decides by the melody between chords: a jump costs its square, which makes one gap affordable and two gaps expensive, and the wide bonus can pay for one but never both. A minor keeps 1 5 9 on its own and takes 1 3 5 here.
