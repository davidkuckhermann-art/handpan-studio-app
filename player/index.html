Transitions

A row can end with a run of single notes that leads into the next chord — the groove stands aside while it plays. Turning Motion on switches it on and turning Motion off switches it off; either way this switch decides.

Chord suggestions

During the break

The click can keep the time for you, or leave you to it. The bass and the drums can carry on so you are playing against a band rather than into silence. The marker either walks the silent bars or waits on the last beat you heard.
