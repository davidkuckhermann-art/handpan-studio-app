The drum kit as its own instrument

The kit leaves the Drums card and gets a card of its own: its own button in the band, its own Play and Sound pages, its own groove browser, its own mixer strip. The percussion card goes back to being only the percussion. Either can play alone or both together, as now; they just no longer share a page. All screens at the phone card's real size.

1 · The band: seven instruments

The kit gets its own button between Percussion and Bass. The two drum buttons are named for what they are: Percussion (the hand drums, today's "Drums") and Drums (the kit). Mute, solo and the staff work for each on its own. On the phone the row has seven tiles instead of six.

2 · The Drums card (the kit)

Play holds what the drummer plays: the groove and the Freedom. Sound holds the kit itself - which kit, the mic mix - and the mix: volume, pan, reverb, effects, as every instrument's card has them.

Questions

  1. Names: "Percussion" (the hand drums, "Perc." in the phone's row) and "Drums" (the kit), as drawn - or keep "Drums" for the percussion and call the kit "Kit"?
  2. Presets: should the kit's card open on a Presets page like the guitar's and piano's (Rock, Pop, Jazz brushes, Hip-hop, House, Trap - each a groove, a kit and a Freedom), or straight on Play as drawn?
  3. A table's own groove (the arrangement editor sets one per table for the percussion): the kit too, beside it?
  4. Band presets (Jazz trio, Pop band...): name the kit or the percussion per preset - e.g. Pop band on the kit, Latin on the percussion?
  5. Swing on Play: show the groove's swing (read-only, set in Drum Grooves) as drawn, or a swing control right on the card?
