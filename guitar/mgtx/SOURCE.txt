The guitar's Metal sounds (David, 2 Oct 2026, on the metal listening page: "that's the best one for sure - integrate it as
metal guitar into the app"): METAL-GTX by Unreal Instruments (紫式部, @Purple_Shikibu_ on X/Twitter),
https://unreal-instruments.wixsite.com/unreal-instruments/metal-gtx - a humbucker guitar recorded DI, F#1-E6, for SFZ.

What is here (downloaded 2 Oct 2026 from the author's Google Drive, UI_METAL-GTX.rar 1,343 MB; converted with ffmpeg):
  sus/  Sus_Down  - sustained down-strokes:   every semitone F#1-E4 (MIDI 30-64), then every second one to E6 (88); 5 s, 0.8 s fade
  pmd/  Mute_Down - palm mutes, down-strokes: every semitone F#1-E4, then every second one to E5 (76); 0.8 s, 0.2 s fade
  pmu/  Mute_Up   - palm mutes, up-strokes:   the same
The first two takes of each note (3 Oct 2026: lighter - 9.9 MB, it was 18 with three takes and 1.5 s palm mutes); STEREO MP3 128 kbps, 44.1 kHz - each file's two channels are two different
performances (correlation about 0, about 7 ms apart): the library's own double-tracking, so the left channel is the left
guitar and the right the right (patches/guitar-metal-gtx.py). map.json per folder: [{lo, hi, c, layers: [[0, 127, [files]]]}].
