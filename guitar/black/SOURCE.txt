The guitar's "Jazz dark" sound (2 Oct 2026, patches/guitar-sound.py): Karoryfer Samples, Black And Green Guitars - the
BLACK guitar, a hollowbody electric. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars (branch main).

LICENCE: CC0-1.0 (public domain dedication). No credit needed, no limits on use inside the app, paid or free.

What is here: the "ord" (ordinary pluck, "twang") recordings, read from the library's own map
Programs/modules/maps_black/ord.sfz - every third semitone from E2 (MIDI 40) to A#5 (82), the three loudness layers
(velocity 0-42, 43-84, 85-127) and two takes of each: 15 zones, 90 files. Converted from the WAV originals to mono MP3
112 kbps, 7 s at most with a 0.8 s fade (_lab/guitar-listen/fetch_sets.py). The notes ring 2-3 s, as recorded.
map.json: [{lo, hi, c, layers: [[lovel, hivel, [files]]]}], MF's format - played by gtrTake / gtrNote.
Chosen by David on the jazz guitar listening page (_lab/guitar-listen/); darker of the two (spectral centroid ~1.1 kHz).
