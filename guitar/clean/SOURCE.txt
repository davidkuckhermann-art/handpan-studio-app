GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins, preset 000-027 "Clean Guitar".
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS (GeneralUser-GS.sf2, branch main).

What is here: every zone of the preset - 9 key zones (centres E2 A2 D3 ... ), four velocity layers (0-59, 60-83, 84-106,
107-127), one take each: 36 files. Pulled with _lab/eguitar-listen/sf2_extract.py: the zone's sample (the left of a stereo
pair) tuned by its corrections, its loop played as a SoundFont player plays it and its volume envelope's hold, decay and
sustain applied, then mono MP3 112 kbps, 7 s with a 0.8 s fade.
map.json: [{lo, hi, c, layers: [[lovel, hivel, [files]]]}], MF's format - played by gtrTake / gtrNote.
