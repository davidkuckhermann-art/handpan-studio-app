The guitar's Metal sounds' samples (David, 2 Oct 2026, on the metal listening page: "let's go with that for now for the metal sound"):
FreePats project, FSBS Electric Guitar Direct - a Fender electric guitar (basic model) sampled DIRECT, the bridge pickup, no amp:
the raw signal, which the app's metal amp (patches/guitar-metal-amp.py) distorts, then a cabinet impulse response (guitar/ir/).
https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ElectricGuitar/ - https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-direct (branch main, "EGuitarFSBS-direct bridge 20220911.sfz").

LICENCE: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). No credit needed, no limits on use inside the app, paid or free.

What is here: read from the library's own SFZ map - every third semitone or so from E2 (MIDI 40) to G#5 (80): 12 zones,
both loudness layers (velocity 0-92 soft, 93-127 full) and all four takes of each: 76 files. Converted from the FLAC
originals to mono MP3 112 kbps, 7 s at most with a 0.8 s fade (_lab/eguitar-listen/_helpers.py, TAKES 4).
map.json: [{lo, hi, c, layers: [[lovel, hivel, [files]]]}], MF's format - played by gtrTake / gtrNote.
