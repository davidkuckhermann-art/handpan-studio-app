The guitar's "Jazz bright" sound (2 Oct 2026, patches/guitar-sound.py): Karoryfer Samples, Black And Green Guitars - the
GREEN guitar, a hollowbody electric. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars (branch main).

LICENCE: CC0-1.0 (public domain dedication). No credit needed, no limits on use inside the app, paid or free.

What is here: the "ord" (ordinary pluck, "twang") recordings, read from the library's own map
Programs/modules/maps_green/ord.sfz - every third semitone from E2 (MIDI 40) to A#5 (82), the three loudness layers
(velocity 0-42, 43-84, 85-127) and every take the library has (2, 4, 4 - two of each until 2 Oct): 15 zones, 150 files. Converted from the WAV originals to mono MP3
112 kbps, 7 s at most with a 0.8 s fade (_lab/guitar-listen/fetch_sets.py).
map.json: [{lo, hi, c, layers: [[lovel, hivel, [files]]]}], MF's format - played by gtrTake / gtrNote.
Chosen by David on the jazz guitar listening page (_lab/guitar-listen/); brighter of the two (spectral centroid ~2.6 kHz); its notes ring 4-5 s.

STACCATO (2 Oct 2026, patches/guitar-stac.py): stac.json and <c>-s1-<n>.mp3 - the library's staccato recordings (plucks
damped by hand, Programs/modules/maps_green/stac.sfz), the same 15 zones, one layer, 4 takes: 60 files. The app plays them
for a pluck the hand damps within 160 ms (chucks, palm mutes, skanks) - David: the chucks were "a bit of a machine gun
effect and ... too soft" on this guitar.
