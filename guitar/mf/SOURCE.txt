The guitar's samples (28 Sep 2026): MF Natural Concert Guitar by Markus Fiedler (Fiedler Audio,
https://www.fiedler-audio.de/creative-commons-library/, file mf-natural-concert-guitar-ogg.zip).

LICENCE: a modified CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 - non-commercial, share-alike; recordings may be used in commercial
music productions, but the library may not be built into commercial sampling products, whole or in part.
So: the Rail (David's working copy) only. NOT for a publish of main or any paid build until Markus Fiedler
has agreed a licence (David is asking him). If he does not, put back the Tone.js set (git: guitar/nylon/,
removed in the commit that added this folder) or the CC0 FreePats set.

What is here: the seven zones from A2 to the 12th fret on high E (MIDI 43-79), each recorded note at four
loudness layers (pp p f ff) with 3 takes (A4: 3/3/5/7, D5: 6 each) - 102 takes, converted from the OGG
originals to mono MP3 112 kbps, trimmed to 7 s with a 0.8 s fade. map.json: [{lo, hi, c, layers:
[[lovel, hivel, [files]]]}], read from the library's own SFZ. Played by gtrTake / gtrNote (patches/guitar-mf.py).
