Does row 2 fit? — measured, not guessed

The pills are drawn at the metrics the app gives them on the phone today — Figtree 12px/600, 33px tall, 9px side padding, measured off the running app at 393pt (Clear 48.8, Generate 85.8). Row 2 now sits inside the card, so it is measured against the card's inner width (the screen's 361 less the card's 10px each side), with the 6px gaps the card's row uses. What it costs the screen

One more row: 38px of pill plus 8px of gap plus the 10px the groove row was owed — 56px between the chord card and the deck. In your screenshot that band is empty from the groove row down to the deck, roughly 500px, so nothing moves and nothing shrinks; the notation and the deck are untouched. In landscape the jam keeps its header and the rows live in the workbench card, so this is portrait-only by construction.
