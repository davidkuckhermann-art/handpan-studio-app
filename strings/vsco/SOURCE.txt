VSCO 2 Community Edition - strings sections (violins, violas, cellos)
Versilian Studios, Sam Gossner - https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) - no credit required, free to ship in the app.

These files are not the originals: strings/make_vsco.py fetches the WAVs and re-cuts them so that every note is heard
at the same moment (20 ms in for spiccato and pizzicato, 80 ms for sustain and tremolo) and at one loudness; the long
notes are the strong layer, with a loop stretch the app crossfades. The originals' file names are an octave low; the
names here carry the measured MIDI note. Chosen by ear on the listening page _lab/strings-listen (25 Oct 2026) over
Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra and two General MIDI soundfonts.
