The drum kit beside the percussion

Three ways to put the new kit (Virtuosity Drums) into the Drums card, beside today's percussion. Either can play alone, or both together. Below them: the kit's own groove browser and the mixer with a Kit strip. All drawn at the phone card's real size.

A · One Play page, two parts

The Play page holds both parts, each with its own switch and groove. A cajón groove under a kit groove is just both switches on. The Sound page gets a volume for each.

C · One groove, two layers

The Play page keeps a single Groove row that names both layers. The browser gains Percussion · Kit · Pairs, where Pairs are ready-made combinations (a cajón with the kit's hats and kick, a frame drum under a brushed-style ballad). This is the closest to today, with the least to learn.
